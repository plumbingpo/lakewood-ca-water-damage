Selecting a Restoration Professional in Lakewood, CA

Home / Blog / Selecting a Restoration Professional in Lakewood, CA

After water damage at a Lakewood, CA property, few choices matter more than which restoration company you hire. That choice affects the speed of the restoration, how completely the damage gets fixed, what comes out of your own pocket, and whether mold or other secondary problems appear months down the road. Not every outfit is qualified, and hiring the wrong one can mean inflated invoices, a job left incomplete, or something worse. Use the points below to tell a strong restoration company apart from a weak one.

Verify IICRC Certification

The Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC) is the gold standard for restoration industry credentials. To earn certification, a technician trains rigorously in restoration science, covering mold prevention, moisture measurement, structural drying, and proper extraction technique. Ask any restoration company you're considering whether their technicians hold current IICRC certifications in Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD). Steer clear of any firm unable to show proof of them.

Ask for Proof of Insurance and Licenses

Any company you let work in your home needs full insurance, meaning general liability as well as workers' compensation. Ask to see the insurance certificates, then check that they have not expired. Without proper coverage, you could be liable for injuries or additional property damage that occur during the restoration. Licensing matters too, so confirm the firm holds whatever business licenses Lakewood or California requires.

Ask How Fast They Respond

Water damage is a time-sensitive emergency. Every hour of delay increases damage and cost. Real 24/7 emergency service, with crews that reach your neighborhood fast, should be part of the deal. Get a specific answer on how long they take to reach Lakewood, and make sure a live person picks up the emergency line instead of voicemail or an answering service that slows things down.

Find Out What Equipment and Methods They Use

Without the right machines, a restoration job cannot be done properly. Ask what type of extraction equipment the company uses (truck-mounted units are superior to portable-only operations), what type of dehumidifiers they deploy (LGR units are the industry standard), and whether they use thermal imaging for moisture detection. Ask how drying is monitored too, because a reputable company checks moisture every day and repositions equipment according to the readings instead of setting it up and walking away.

Read Reviews and Call References

Online reviews provide valuable insight into a company's track record. Search Yelp, Google, and the BBB to see if customer satisfaction, professional conduct, and work quality hold up consistently. Focus on comments covering the finished quality of the work, insurance assistance, whether crews were on time, and how well the company communicated. Feel free to request references from water damage jobs the company finished recently in Lakewood.

Understand Pricing and Insurance Billing

Expect a detailed estimate up front, along with a plain explanation of the pricing, from any firm worth hiring. Xactimate and similar industry-standard pricing programs, recognized and accepted by insurance carriers, are used by many quality contractors. Ask whether the company works directly with insurance carriers and whether they can bill your insurance directly. A demand for big upfront money, or a refusal to deal with your insurer, is a warning sign.
