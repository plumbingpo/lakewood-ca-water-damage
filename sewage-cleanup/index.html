Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Lakewood Restoration Co provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Lakewood, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Know the location and condition of your sewer cleanout for emergency access
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs

Our Sewage Cleanup Process

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle sewage cleanup from start to finish:

1

Hazard Check and Containment

We start by measuring how far the sewage contamination reaches, noting any safety hazards and setting up containment so clean areas are not cross-contaminated.

2

Extracting Sewage and Removing Materials

All standing sewage is extracted. Carpet and its padding, insulation, drywall and other contaminated porous items are removed, then disposed of properly as biohazard waste.

3

Cleaning and Disinfecting

We thoroughly clean every surface that remains, then disinfect it with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution. Meanwhile, air scrubbers fitted with HEPA filters pull pathogens and other contaminants from the air.

4

Drying & Odor Elimination

Commercial drying equipment removes all residual moisture. Industrial deodorization eliminates sewage odors. Clearance testing confirms the space is safe for occupancy.
